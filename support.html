Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I unlock Pro?
    Open the Editor tab, tap the "..." button in the top-right corner, then tap "Get Pro". Pro is a one-time purchase ($1.99), not a subscription.
  • How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
    Open Settings and tap "Restore Purchase", or use the button on the purchase screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.
  • Where is my data stored?
    All expressions, history, and settings are stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.
  • Which cron engines are supported?
    Linux (free), plus Quartz, Spring, Kubernetes, AWS EventBridge, Jenkins, and GitHub Actions with Pro.
